An on-vehicle AI operating system that moves ADAS from reactive to predictive: a shared world model, a deterministic safety kernel, Vietnamese-language alerts the driver can understand, and XGBoost vehicle-health intelligence. Everything runs on the edge box, nothing round-trips to a server.

I'm an AIoT engineering student at IUH. Day to day that means AI systems, embedded work and digital IC design, plus the startup side: I founded CareID and Cognita Education, and I mentor teams at Startup Runway and the Tsinghua Global SDG case competition.

What I like is the part where a model has to survive contact with hardware. Quantizing YOLOv8 until it fits a Jetson Nano's power budget. Getting a DMA state machine through synthesis. Keeping a fast path under 100 ms when CAN traffic and an LLM are fighting for the same box.

The product side matters to me for the same reason. Before CareID had a working screen I had already run 30+ interviews and collected 47 sign-ups, because a health passport nobody wants is just a schema. I've also built the financial models, the go-to-market plans and the unit economics behind three of the projects above.

Resourceful mostly out of necessity: with no laptop around I'll SSH into the Jetson from a Galaxy A12 over Termux just to check on a training run.
